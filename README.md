# Program Status Board

Keep your projects moving. Remember everything you've accomplished. Add a project, give a quick update when something moves, and the board keeps your achievements and drafts your weekly summary.

**See the page:** https://wei-wei-hu.github.io/program-management/

![Program Status Board: the opening section with a milestone timeline](cover.png)

## How it works

Four steps, each one feeding the next.

1. **My projects** (any time). Track active projects with an objective, a red, yellow or green status and the next action. Describe a project in your own words and let AI set it up, or fill in a short form. No progress percentages to guess.
2. **Quick update** (under a minute). Answer four questions: what did you accomplish, what's next, what's blocking you, what help do you need. The board marks tasks done, adds what's next and records blockers. You review every change before it is saved.
3. **Achievements** (automatic). Finished milestones, completed tasks and wins from your updates, kept by month. Draft the achievements section of your performance review from your own record.
4. **Weekly summary** (end of week). Accomplishments, unfinished work and upcoming priorities, plus an AI brief for your manager, a client or yourself. Copy it as text or as a formatted table.

![The four steps](steps.png)

## Why it is different

Most project tools focus on tasks, assignments and deadlines. This one also keeps the link between your work, your progress and your own contribution, so after a few months you can answer:

- What have I accomplished across all my projects?
- Which projects have stalled, and why?
- What did I contribute this quarter?
- What are my most important unfinished commitments?
