01 · Weekly loop

Four tabs, used in order each week

Planning on Monday feeds the record on Friday, and the record feeds the update. Nothing is typed twice.

Each morning

Today

What is due, who you are waiting on, and your own open work. Optional 30-second daily report.

Monday · all week

Plan & track

Describe a project in your own words. AI drafts milestones and this week’s tasks; you review, then tick them off.

Friday

Weekly record

One row per task: progress, evidence someone can check, the blocker and the next step.

Friday · month end

Reports

A weekly or monthly update drafted from the record. Pass three checks, then copy it as text or a table.

02 · Status rule

One rule sets every color

Every project is judged the same way, so a red on one project means the same as a red on another.

StatusG · On track

Green

On track. The next milestone date holds.

No open blockers

StatusY · Slipping

Yellow

Slipping, with a plan to recover that you control.

Behind plan · recovery step named

StatusR · Needs a decision

Red

Blocked, or a decision is needed from someone else.

Needs a decision · owner and date

03 · What it handles

Built for running several projects at once

Plan by talking

Type or dictate the project the way you would explain it to a colleague. Dates become milestones; tasks land in the right week.

Milestones per project

Each project shows what is done, what is current and what comes next, with late milestones flagged.

Dependencies on people

Record who you are waiting on, the deadline and the escalation path. The Today tab tells you when to follow up.

Weeks that roll forward

Move back and forward by week. Unfinished tasks carry into the current week on their own.

Fact-checked updates

Before sending, confirm every number comes from the record and nothing in progress is reported as done.

Shared board

Saves automatically to one board for the people you invite. Private until you share it.
