01 · Weekly loop

Four tabs, used in order each week

Planning at the start of the week feeds the record at the end of the week, and the record feeds the update. Nothing is typed twice.

Each morning

Today

What is due, who you are waiting on, and your own open work. Optional 30-second daily report.

All week

Plan & track

Describe a project in your own words. AI drafts milestones and this week’s tasks; you review, then tick them off.

End of week

Weekly record

One row per task: progress, evidence someone can check, the blocker and the next step.

End of month

Reports

A weekly or monthly update drafted from the record. Pass three checks, then copy it as text or a table.

02 · What it handles

Keep several projects moving without losing the details

Plan by talking

Describe a project the way you would explain it to a colleague. Turn dates into milestones, actions into tasks, and put the work into the right weeks.

Know where every project stands

See what is done, what is happening now, and what comes next. Late milestones and unfinished work stay visible.

Track who you are waiting on

Record the person, what you need from them, the due date, and when to escalate. See when it is time to follow up.

Carry unfinished work forward

Move from week to week without rebuilding your plan. Open work follows you into the current week so it does not disappear.

Prepare reliable updates

Create updates from the actual project record. Check the numbers and keep work in progress separate from completed results before you send.

Keep everyone on the same page

Work from one shared board with the people you invite. Keep it private until you are ready to share.
