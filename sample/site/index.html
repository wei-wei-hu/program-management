What it does

Built from how status reports go wrong

Talk instead of typing

Describe the project the way you would to a colleague. Dates become milestones and speech-to-text slips get fixed.

Red, yellow, green by one rule

Every project gets its color the same way, so projects can be compared. Override it only with a short reason.

Never stall on someone else

Note who you are waiting on, the deadline and where to escalate. The Today tab tells you when to chase.

Weeks that roll forward

Step back and forward through weeks. Unfinished tasks move into this week on their own.

Updates that stick to facts

Before you send, check that every number comes from the record and nothing in progress is written as done.

Shared with your team

Everything saves automatically to one board for the people you invite. Only you see it until you share it.
